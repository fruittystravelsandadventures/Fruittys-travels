About

A country girl at heart, planning trips across the island she grew up on.

Fruitty's Travel & Adventures is built on knowing Jamaica firsthand — not just booking it. Every recommendation comes from a real visit, a real conversation with the people who run the place, or a client's honest feedback.

Behind the business: years in customer service, a young daughter to build a future for, and a running project to visit 100 spots across Jamaica and give an honest read on which ones are actually worth the trip.
